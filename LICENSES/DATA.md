The original MA-Bench release declares `license: cc-by-4.0` in its supplied README.
The complete original notice and citation are preserved in
`data/heerbench_v05/provenance/MA_Bench_README.md`.

MA-Bench authors are Rodney Lafuente-Mercado, Robert Sincero, Phil Sage and Yuan Xue.
The source publication is *MA-Bench: Measuring Operational Planning in Language Models*,
arXiv:2610.19138 (2026), https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.19138.

adaptation ledger are supplied as attributed derivative benchmark material under

dependency annotation, error-check association and corpus consolidation.
