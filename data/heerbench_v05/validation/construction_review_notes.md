# Construction review notes

These are substantive internal construction checks. They are not independent military-expert validation or experimental results. The resulting records remain pending the new expert review.

## Corrections applied during construction

| Issue | Construction correction |
|---|---|
| Stated instructions or supplied assessments can look inferential in an original rubric. | Retrieval remains A. A distinct relationship or practical consequence is required for B or F. The accepted case remains recorded separately. |
| A fact can be relevant to a scenario without contributing to a particular chain. | Removed decorative prerequisites and source facets. For example, a carrier-package inventory was removed from a chain whose assessment concerned only separately located MEU and amphibious shipping. |
| Several premises jointly support a judgement. | Retained ALL dependency sets and avoided treating each incoming arrow as independently sufficient. |
| Authority or coordination roles sometimes affect the action rather than the prior capability assessment. | Moved those A inputs to the joint prerequisite set of F where their role is actually required. |
| Eleven days of fuel coverage was compared with a fourteen-day replenishment cycle. | Preserved the interval comparison, but did not infer an actual three-day stockout without cycle phase or the next delivery date. The consequence requires a dated stock balance and a sourced remedy for any demonstrated gap. |
| A runway can meet neither sustained nor contingency criteria, with different deficits. | Kept the packet's sustained C-130 criterion distinct from semi-prepared and emergency criteria. Did not transfer a lower threshold to the sustained air-bridge judgement. |
| Communications availability and confidentiality are different constructs. | Separated the OPSEC exposure chain from service disruption and navigation-reliability assessments. |
| A platform description alone does not establish a complete defensive coverage gap. | Required coverage verification and correction of any identified gap, without claiming the whole system lacks coverage. |
| A resource duration needs a valid start date. | Qualified the D+28 support-lapse estimate when the allocation clock was not explicit. Preserved the independently supported duration mismatch. |
| Later scenario orders may supersede earlier assumptions. | Distinguished the current EXORD from earlier planning guidance and required revalidation of inherited phase estimates or permissions. |
| A criterion can hide several independent omissions. | Split independent factual and action requirements while retaining genuine relational propositions as single criteria. |
| A reference may over-prescribe one solution. | Added acceptable alternatives and kept unsupported quantities, technical thresholds, unallocated assets and ungranted permissions out of mandatory references. |

## Deterministic validation

The final validation checks the complete original item universe, immutable original fields, the accepted screening baseline, unique identifiers, normalized scoring records, reciprocal item/question mappings, complete chain membership, chain-local dependency direction, source ranges and checksums, negative-check applicability records, and reconstruction of all ten model-visible source packets.

Five corruption probes were also used while strengthening the validator. Duplicate question IDs, a removed chain dependency, a modified source packet, a duplicate negative-check pair and a modified normalized criterion were all detected. These are checks of the validation code, not estimates of annotation quality.

The spreadsheet summary uses formulas over its review tables. Exported totals are compared with the canonical summary. Representative regions of every worksheet are rendered and inspected for readability. Prompt construction is checked for model/evaluator separation, preservation of full scenario text and enforcement of complete joint parent sets.

## Remaining adjudication

Military reviewers should inspect each complete chain, then its questions, reference passages, scoring criteria and alternatives. Partial original-item reuse and construction exclusions merit explicit review. An item can be restored if a sound complete chain is supplied. The release does not claim that every conceivable valid chain has been found, that all accepted alternatives have been exhausted, or that original rubric-item coverage is a measure of military representativeness.

Any semantic edit should be applied to every affected chain before the next version is frozen. Model pilots, controlled error interventions, reference-supported comparison and judge calibration follow that adjudication.
